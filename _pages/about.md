Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). He is supervised by Professor Junxian He. Previously, Professor Junxian He also advised Junteng Liu during his undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).

He graduated with a B.Eng. in June 2024 from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where he completed his undergraduate studies (2020-2024) and received the prestigious Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

His research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML). His specific research interests include:
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
