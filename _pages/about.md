About Me
======
I am a first-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group in the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Professor Junxian He. Previously, I graduated with a B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and Interpretability.
