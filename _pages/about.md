Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, part of the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). He is supervised by Professor Junxian He. Professor Junxian He also previously advised Junteng Liu during his undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).

Junteng completed his B.Eng. (2020-2024) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, graduating in June 2024, where he also received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship. 

His research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. 
His specific research interests include:
* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
* **LLM truthfulness and Interpretability**
